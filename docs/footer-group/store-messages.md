title: Store Messages
sidebar_position: 59
---

The **Store Messages** section displays persistent promotional or informational banners at the top of your store (e.g., free shipping, site-wide discounts, or return policy highlights). It uses icon-text pairs to communicate key messages clearly and visually.

All configuration options for this section are managed through its child block:

- **Store Messages Block**: Full settings and customization options are available in **[Blocks → Composite Function Block → Store Messages](/blocks/composite-function-block/store-messages)**.
