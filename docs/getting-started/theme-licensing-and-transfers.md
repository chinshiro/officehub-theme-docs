# Theme License and Transfer

According to [Shopify’s Terms of Service, Section 9.4](https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms#94-theme-store), you must have a **valid license** to use our theme in your store. The **only way** to obtain a valid license is by purchasing the theme directly from the **official Shopify Theme Store**.

## One License, One Store

A single theme license is valid for **one Shopify store only**.  
For example, if you purchased the theme for `my-first-store.myshopify.com`, it may only be used on that store.

You may only use the theme on a different store **after officially transferring the license**—a process governed by Shopify.

:::caution Important
Scenario does **not** offer refunds, discounts, or bulk theme purchases. All theme sales and licensing are managed exclusively by Shopify.
:::


## How to Check Your License

If you purchased the theme from the **Shopify Theme Store**, you likely have a valid license. To confirm:

- Contact **Shopify Support** and ask them to verify your license, **or**
- Reach out to our [support team](/contacting-support) with your store URL.

---

## What If My Store Doesn’t Have a Valid License?

If you obtained the theme from **any source other than the Shopify Theme Store**, your store is likely using an **unauthorized (unlicensed) copy**.

Consequences include:

- ❌ No access to **support** or **theme updates**
- ⚠️ Risk of **legal action**: We reserve the right to report your store to Shopify’s legal team and file a **DMCA takedown notice**, which could result in your store being suspended.

> ✅ **To avoid disruption**, please either:
> - Purchase the theme from the [Shopify Theme Store](https://themes.shopify.com), **or**
> - Completely remove our theme from your store.

---

## Can I Import the Theme from Another Store?

You may only import the theme into another store **if that store has its own valid license** purchased from the Shopify Theme Store.

Simply copying or transferring theme files between stores **without a license** violates Shopify’s Terms of Service.

---

## What If I Operate Multiple Markets?

- ✅ **Same Shopify store, multiple markets** (e.g., US and EU storefronts under one admin):  
  → Only **one license** is required.

- ❌ **Separate Shopify stores** for each market:  
  → You must purchase **one license per store**.

---

## Agencies and Development Stores

If you’re an agency building stores for clients, you may use a **password-protected development store** to test and customize the theme—**at no extra cost**—provided that:

- The development store **does not accept real payments**
- The store is **not publicly accessible** (i.e., password-protected)

### Important Notes for Agencies:

1. The theme **must be purchased on the live (production) store**, not the development store.
2. When contacting our support team about a development store, **always include the URL of the licensed (live) store** in your message. This helps us verify your license quickly.

---

## How to Transfer a License

License transfers are **handled exclusively by Shopify**. To transfer a theme license from one store to another:

1. Contact **Shopify Support** directly for instructions.
2. Be aware that **Shopify typically requires the original store to be fully closed** before a license can be transferred.

> ❗ As of now, **Shopify does not allow license transfers while keeping the original store active**. If you still need to operate the original store, a transfer is not possible—and you’ll need to purchase a new license for the second store.

---

For any licensing questions, please contact [Shopify Support](https://help.shopify.com/) or our [support team](/contacting-support) with your licensed store URL.
