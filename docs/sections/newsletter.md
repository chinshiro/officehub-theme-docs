The **Newsletter** section collects visitor email addresses to grow your marketing list and enable ongoing customer engagement through email campaigns. It includes a heading, descriptive text, and a fully functional subscription form that integrates with your store’s email marketing provider.

For full configuration details—including layout width, background styling, and child block settings (heading, text, form)—see:  
**[Blocks → Composite Function Block → Newsletter](/blocks/composite-function-block/newsletter)**
