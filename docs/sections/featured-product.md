The **Featured Product** section displays a single product with the same rich, interactive experience as a full product detail page—including media gallery, variant picker, price, description, and buy buttons. Use it to highlight hero items, bestsellers, or new arrivals anywhere on your store (e.g., homepage, landing pages, or collections).

For full configuration details—including gallery layout, variant controls, buy button styling, collapsible tabs, complementary products, and quick-buy integration—see:  
**[Blocks → Composite Function Block → Featured Product](/blocks/composite-function-block/featured-product)**
