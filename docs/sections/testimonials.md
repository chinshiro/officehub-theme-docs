The **Testimonials** section displays customer reviews, endorsements, or user-generated content in a visually engaging carousel format. It’s a powerful tool for building trust, showcasing social proof, and reinforcing credibility across your store—especially on homepages, product pages, or landing sections.

For full configuration details—including autoplay settings, testimonial card styling, media support (image/video/3D model), rating stars, and child block options (heading, text, video, image)—see:  
**[Blocks → Composite Function Block → Testimonials](/blocks/composite-function-block/testimonials)**
