The **Slideshow** section creates a full-featured, responsive carousel banner that supports **images**, **videos**, and **3D models** as slide content. It’s ideal for hero sections, promotional campaigns, or immersive storytelling at the top of your homepage or landing pages.

For full configuration details—including slide effects, autoplay, navigation controls, pagination styling, overlay settings, and child block options (heading, text, button)—see:  
**[Blocks → Composite Function Block → Slideshow](/blocks/composite-function-block/slideshow)**
