The **Video With Text** section combines a video with descriptive content—including a heading, rich text, and call-to-action button—in a flexible layout. It supports multiple arrangements: text overlaid on the video, or text positioned to the left or right. Ideal for product demos, brand stories, explainer content, or full-viewport background video experiences.

For full configuration details—including layout options, overlay opacity, play button settings, video width, and child block controls—see:  
**[Blocks → Composite Function Block → Video With Text](/blocks/composite-function-block/video-with-text)**
