The **Blog Sidebar** template allows you to create blog index pages with a dedicated sidebar—perfect for featuring popular posts, tags, newsletter signups, or related content alongside your article list.

## How to Set Up

1. **Start from the Default Blog template**  
   Use the standard blog layout as your foundation.

2. **Hide the default Blog Posts Lists block**  
   In the main section, locate and **hide or remove** the `Blog posts lists` block to avoid duplication.

3. **Add a Sidebar Section**  
   Insert the **[Sidebar Section](/sections/sidebar-section)** into your page layout.

4. **Configure the Sidebar**  
   Inside the **Sidebar Section**, use the **Sidebar block** to add dynamic content such as:
   - **Blog tags** (with auto-population from current blog)  
   - **Recent articles**  
   - **Newsletter form**  
   - **Social links**, **contact info**, or **featured collections**  
   > Full configuration options: **[Sidebar Section](/sections/sidebar-section)**

5. **Re-add Blog Posts Lists inside the Sidebar Section**  
   To ensure your blog post grid appears **next to the sidebar** (not below it), drag the **Blog Posts Lists** block into the **main content area of the Sidebar Section** (not inside the sidebar column itself).

6. **Enhance with Additional Blocks**  
   The **Sidebar Section** fully supports all `@theme` blocks in both the main content area and the sidebar column, giving you complete flexibility to build a rich, engaging blog layout.

> 💡 **Tip**: Use the **“Hidden on mobile”** option in sidebar child blocks to optimize space on small screens, or enable **“Collapse”** mobile layout for an accordion-style experience.
