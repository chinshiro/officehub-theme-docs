The **Page Sidebar** template enables you to create content pages with a dedicated sidebar—ideal for “About Us,” “Contact,” or resource pages where you want to display supplementary content (e.g., contact forms, links, or calls-to-action) alongside your main text.

## How to Set Up

1. **Start from the Default Page template**  
   Use the standard page layout as your base.

2. **Hide the default Page Body Text block**  
   In the main section, locate and **hide or remove** the `Page body text` block to prevent duplicate content.

3. **Add a Sidebar Section**  
   Insert the **[Sidebar Section](/sections/sidebar-section)** into your page layout.

4. **Configure the Sidebar**  
   Inside the **Sidebar Section**, use the **Sidebar block** to add your desired sidebar content:
   - Contact form  
   - Navigation links  
   - Blog tags  
   - Product or collection lists  
   - Social media, payment icons, etc.  
   > Full settings: **[Sidebar Section](/sections/sidebar-section)**

5. **Re-add Page Body Text inside the Sidebar Section**  
   To ensure your page content appears **next to the sidebar** (not below it), drag the **Page Body Text** block **into the main area of the Sidebar Section** (not inside the sidebar column itself).

6. **Enhance with Additional Blocks**  
   The **Sidebar Section** fully supports all `@theme` blocks in both the main content area and the sidebar column, giving you complete layout flexibility.

> 💡 **Tip**: Use the **Sidebar Section’s mobile layout settings** to control how the sidebar appears on small screens (grid or collapsible accordion).
