The **Article Sidebar** template enables you to create blog post pages with a dedicated sidebar—ideal for displaying related content such as author info, popular articles, tags, newsletter signups, or social sharing alongside your article.

## How to Set Up

1. **Start from the Default Blog Post template**  
   Use the standard blog post layout as your base.

2. **Hide the default Article Body Text block**  
   In the main section, locate and **hide or remove** the `Article body text` block to prevent duplicate content rendering.

3. **Add a Sidebar Section**  
   Insert the **[Sidebar Section](/sections/sidebar-section)** into your page layout.

4. **Configure the Sidebar**  
   Inside the **Sidebar Section**, use the **Sidebar block** to add dynamic content such as:
   - **Blog tags** (auto-populated from the current article)  
   - **Recent or related articles**  
   - **Author bio**, **social links**, or **sharing buttons**  
   - **Newsletter form** or **featured products**  
   > Full settings: **[Sidebar Section](/sections/sidebar-section)**

5. **Re-add Article Body Text inside the Sidebar Section**  
   To ensure your article content appears **next to the sidebar** (not below it), drag the **Article Body Text** block into the **main content area of the Sidebar Section** (not inside the sidebar column itself).

6. **Enhance with Additional Blocks**  
   The **Sidebar Section** fully supports all `@theme` blocks in both the main content area and the sidebar column, giving you complete flexibility to build a rich, engaging reading experience.

> 💡 **Tip**: Use the **“Hidden on mobile”** option for non-essential sidebar blocks to prioritize readability on small screens, or enable **“Collapse”** mobile layout for an accordion-style navigation.
