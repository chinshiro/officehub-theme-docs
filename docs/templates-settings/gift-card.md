The **Gift Card** template displays the gift card redemption page (e.g., `/gift_cards/abc123`). It allows customers to view and use their gift card balance.

## Settings

<img src={require('/img/235.webp').default} alt="Gift Card settings" style={{ float: 'left', marginRight: '30px' }} />
- **Gift card image**: Upload a custom image to represent your gift card. If left unset, Shopify’s default gift card graphic will be displayed.
