### Option values

- **Background repeat** (`bg_method`): Tile/repeat (`repeat`), Repeat horizontally (`center top repeat-x`), Repeat vertically (`center top repeat-y`), Top center, no repeat (`center top no-repeat`)

> 💡 Available under **Theme settings** in the editor.
