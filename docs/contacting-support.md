We’re here to help! As a certified **Shopify Theme Partner**, we provide free support for:

- ✅ Bug fixes  
- ✅ Theme installation & setup  
- ✅ Clarification on built-in features  

We do **not** include the following in the theme price:

- ❌ Custom development  
- ❌ App integrations

## Support Policy

As a Shopify Theme Partner, we respond within 1–2 business days.

All support is provided to the original purchaser of the theme.  
For customizations, speed optimizations, or advanced help, we offer paid services — just mention it in your message!

> 💡 **Before editing code**: Always **duplicate your theme** in Shopify.  
> 🔧 **Not comfortable with code?** Consider hiring a [Shopify Expert](https://experts.shopify.com).
