✅ Key Features​

⚡ Performance & SEO Optimized​

  • Lightning Fast: Optimized for Core Web Vitals to ensure top performance and improved SEO rankings.
  • Mobile-First Responsive Design: Flawless display across all devices — from smartphones to desktops.

🎨 Design & Customization​

  • Fully Customizable: Over 200+ theme settings with a Style Schemes system, plus 30+ sections and blocks for limitless design flexibility.
  • Hotspot Scene Imagery: Showcase products in real-life contexts with interactive hotspot overlays that highlight key features.
  • Sidebar Layout Support: Add sidebars to Pages, Blog Index, and Blog Post templates for enhanced content organization.
  • 3D Model Integration: Embed GLB 3D models on any page type — ideal for product demos, furniture, or tech gear.

🌍 Global & Multilingual Ready​

  • Multi-language & Multi-currency Ready: Sell globally with built-in support for multiple languages and currencies.
  • One-Click Auto Translation: Free integration with Microsoft Translator (80+ languages) and Google Translate (130+ languages) — no app required.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Accessibility & Inclusivity​

  • Accessibility Compliant: Meets WCAG 2.1 AA standards for inclusive shopping experiences.

💼 B2B & Business Features​

  • B2B Features: Built-in quantity rules, volume pricing, and wholesale-ready functionality.
  • Multi-Store Google Maps: Display multiple store locations on an interactive map with search and address lookup.

📝 Content & Blog Experience​

  • Auto-Generated Article Table of Contents: Blog posts automatically generate a clickable directory tree from headings (H1–H6) for easy navigation.
  • Blog Posts Section: Integrated blog with articles on office design, ergonomics, and productivity — enhances SEO and brand authority.

🔥 Conversion & Marketing Tools​

  • Predictive Search with Hot Links: Enable live search suggestions as users type, plus customizable “Popular Links” for quick access.
  • Countdown Timer: Create urgency with a fully customizable countdown component for flash sales, launches, or limited offers.

🛠️ Technical Excellence​

  • One-Stop Shopping Experience: Highlights variety, low prices, fast shipping, and bulk discounts.
  • Social Proof Elements: Showcases stats like 130+ countries served, 99% positive reviews, and 350k+ products sold.
  • Lifestyle & Visual Storytelling: High-quality images of real office spaces, desks, chairs, and organized work environments to inspire customers.

🎯 Ideal For​

  • Office supply retailers
  • Furniture & workspace brands
  • B2B & wholesale businesses
  • Brands focused on productivity, ergonomics, and modern design
